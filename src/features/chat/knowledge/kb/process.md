# Process: The 14-Day Launch Plan

- Day 1–2 — Audit & Discovery: Deep dive into the current setup, goals, and competitive landscape.
- Day 3–5 — Strategy Blueprint: Custom roadmap with channels, messaging, and conversion plan.
- Day 6–10 — Build & Tracking: Website updates, ad setup, tracking pixels, and landing pages.
- Day 11–14 — Launch & Optimise: Go live, monitor performance, and optimise for results.

Most projects launch within 14 days. Larger builds may take 4–6 weeks depending on scope.

## What clients need to get started
- Access to existing website and hosting
- Brand assets (logo, colours, images)
- Google Analytics and ad account access
- Key business goals and target audience info
- Availability for a 30-minute kickoff call

## What to expect in the first month
A complete strategy, live campaigns, tracking in place, and early performance data to guide optimisation.

## Reporting
Weekly performance summaries and monthly deep-dive reports with actionable insights.
