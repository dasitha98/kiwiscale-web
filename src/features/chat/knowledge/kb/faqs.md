# FAQs

## How long does a typical project take?
Most projects launch within 14 days. Larger builds may take 4–6 weeks depending on scope.

## How does pricing work?
KiwiScale offers project-based pricing with clear deliverables. No hidden fees. A detailed quote is provided after the strategy call.

## What industries do you work with?
Professional services, trades, e-commerce, and SaaS — but the systems work across industries.

## What do you actually provide?
Strategy, design, development, SEO, paid advertising, content, and ongoing optimisation — everything needed under one roof.

## How often do I get reports?
Weekly performance summaries and monthly deep-dive reports with actionable insights.

## What tools and platforms do you use?
Google Analytics 4, Tag Manager, Meta Ads, Google Ads, Search Console, and modern CMS platforms.

## What should I expect in the first month?
A complete strategy, live campaigns, tracking in place, and early performance data to guide optimisation.

## Do you guarantee results?
KiwiScale doesn't make empty guarantees. It commits to transparent reporting, continuous optimisation, and measurable progress towards goals.

## Can I see my ad and analytics accounts?
Yes. Clients own all accounts and data. Full transparency is a core value.

## What if I already have a website?
KiwiScale audits the existing site and recommends improvements or a rebuild based on what will drive the best results.
